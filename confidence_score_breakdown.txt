================================================================================
ECHOASSIST — CONFIDENCE SCORE & SIGNAL QUALITY THRESHOLD EXPLANATION
================================================================================

1. HOW THE CONFIDENCE SCORE IS CALCULATED (STEP-BY-STEP)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The confidence score measures how strongly the neural network identifies the winning 
acoustic class across the entire breathing recording.

Step 1: 5-Second Window Segmentation
   The audio signal y (sampled at 4000 Hz) is divided into 5-second non-overlapping 
   breath cycle windows (20,000 samples each).

Step 2: Neural Network Multi-Label Inferences
   Each 5-second window is passed through ResNet18. The model outputs two independent 
   sigmoids: p_crackle and p_wheeze. 
   
   These are converted into joint probabilities across the 4 sound categories for window i:
     - P_i(both)    = p_crackle * p_wheeze
     - P_i(crackle) = p_crackle * (1 - p_wheeze)
     - P_i(wheeze)  = (1 - p_crackle) * p_wheeze
     - P_i(normal)  = (1 - p_crackle) * (1 - p_wheeze)

Step 3: Time-Averaging Across Windows
   The probabilities from all 5-second windows are averaged:
     mean_probs = np.mean(all_probs, axis=0)

Step 4: Winning Class & Confidence Score Extraction
   - top_idx = np.argmax(mean_probs)
   - label   = CLASSES[top_idx]
   - confidence = float(mean_probs[top_idx])

   The Confidence Score is simply the numerical value of the top class's mean 
   probability (ranging from 0.00 to 1.00, or 0% to 100%).


================================================================================
2. CONFIDENCE WINDOWS & SIGNAL QUALITY THRESHOLDS
================================================================================

Because there are 4 mutually exclusive classes, a purely random guess produces 
a baseline probability of 0.25 (25%). The confidence score is categorized into 
three operational windows:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WINDOW 1: BAD / UNUSABLE AUDIO (Refusal Window)
Range:         0.00 to 0.15 (0% to 15%)
Threshold:     confidence < 0.15 (CONF_THR)
System Status: REFUSED (Graceful refusal triggered)
Explanation:   The top probability is lower than random chance (below 15%). The audio 
               is either flatline silence, corrupted static, or severe noise. 
               The system stops processing and displays:
               "Signal quality too low (confidence=X.XX). Re-record."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WINDOW 2: POOR QUALITY / MODERATE UNCERTAINTY WINDOW
Range:         0.15 to 0.30 (15% to 30%)
System Status: PROCESSED, tagged as [poor quality]
Explanation:   The signal contains detectable audio features, but the probability 
               margin is narrow (near baseline random chance). The system processes 
               the recording, displays the prediction, spectrogram, and Grad-CAM 
               heatmap, but warns the user that quality is poor.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WINDOW 3: GOOD QUALITY / HIGH CONFIDENCE WINDOW
Range:         0.30 to 1.00 (30% to 100%)
System Status: PROCESSED, tagged as [good]
Explanation:   The neural network has identified strong acoustic patterns (such as 
               distinct high-frequency wheeze streaks or sharp crackle spikes). 
               The prediction is processed with high confidence and tagged as [good].
